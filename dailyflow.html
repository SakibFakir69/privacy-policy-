DailyFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

DailyFlow ("the app") is developed by Seven Venture Labs. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our app.

Data Storage

DailyFlow stores all your tasks, habits, and settings locally on your device using SQLite. No personal data is sent to any server. We have no backend, no accounts, and no cloud sync.

Advertising

DailyFlow uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

DailyFlow offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads, processed securely through Google Play. We do not store any payment information.

Permissions

The app requests notification permissions to send you task and habit reminders. These notifications are generated locally and no notification data is sent to external servers.

Backup & Restore

The JSON backup feature exports your data as a local file on your device. This file is never uploaded anywhere automatically.

Children

DailyFlow is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
